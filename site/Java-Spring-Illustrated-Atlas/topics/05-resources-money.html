Resource ownership and decimal money

Prerequisites: 01-ecosystem

Goal & mental model verified

Garbage collection does not replace deterministic resource closure. AutoCloseable integrates with try-with-resources. BigDecimal models decimal arithmetic, with explicit precision/rounding decisions and scale-sensitive equals.

Engineering decision synthesis

Use decimal values or an explicitly specified minor-unit model for premiums. State currency, scale and rounding at business boundaries; neither approach chooses the policy for you.

[S09] [S10]

Pitfall & diagnosis synthesis

BigDecimal constructed from a binary double can inherit representation artifacts. Leaking file/database resources can exhaust capacity even when heap usage appears healthy.

[S09] [S10]

Improve & validate synthesis

Define resource ownership and close scope. Exercise rounding edges and failure paths; compare 1.0 and 1.00 with the intended numeric or representation semantics.
