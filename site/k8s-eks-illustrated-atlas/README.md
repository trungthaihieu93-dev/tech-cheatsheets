# Kubernetes + AWS EKS Illustrated Atlas

Open `index.html` for the complete offline atlas. Open `topics/*.html` for individual lessons. Diagrams are exact editable SVGs in `figures/`; narrow screens can scroll figures to keep labels readable.

Depth 2: Best practices. 14 topics, 22 diagrams, 39 primary sources. Two vendor-published customer cases plus one hypothetical insurance scenario. Click each comprehension question to reveal its answer.

`quick-reference.html` contains decision tables and a glossary. `examples/` contains reviewed YAML teaching specimens and explicit adaptation notes. `pack.json` is editable lesson content; `sources.json` is the source/claim ledger. `manifest.json` records hashes of packaged files.

Research was retrieved on 10 October 2026. Living documentation and feature support must be checked for the actual target cluster, region and compute mode. Examples were not deployed; no benchmark or cost-saving result was measured.
